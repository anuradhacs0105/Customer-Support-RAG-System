ORDER TRACKING

Customers can track their order by logging into their account and opening the My Orders section.

To track an order, select the required order and click Track Order.

Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to become available after shipment confirmation.


ORDER CANCELLATION

Customers can cancel an order before it has been shipped.

To cancel an order, log in to the account and open My Orders.

Select the order and choose Cancel Order.

Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may not be possible.


REFUND POLICY

Refunds are processed after the returned product has been received and inspected.

The refund may take several business days to appear in the original payment method.


RETURN POLICY

Customers can request returns for eligible products within the specified return period.

The product should be unused and returned in acceptable condition.

Customers should open the My Orders section and select the Return option for the applicable order.


PAYMENT METHODS

Customers can use supported debit cards, credit cards, and net banking.

Available payment methods may depend on the customer's location.


DELIVERY

Delivery time depends on the customer's location and selected delivery option.

Customers can view the estimated delivery date during checkout and in the order details.


ACCOUNT SUPPORT

Customers can update their account information from the account settings page.

If a customer cannot access their account, they should use the password reset option.


DAMAGED PRODUCT

Customers who receive a damaged product should contact customer support and provide the order details.

The support team may request photographs or additional information to process the complaint.
